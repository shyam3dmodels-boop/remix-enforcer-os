# UI/UX Designer Skill

## Aesthetic Identity: Kamakura Summer Sky × HarmonyOS Liquid Glass
1. **Visual Hierarchy & Depth**:
   - Background: Crisp summer sky palettes (deep azure, cyan glow, cumulus white clouds).
   - Surfaces: Liquid glass cards with multi-layer blur (`backdrop-blur-2xl`), translucent white tinted fills (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` to `0.75`), and delicate border strokes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`).
   - Shadows: Soft, diffuse ambient glows (`rgba(35, 105, 201, 0.08)`) with zero harsh solid black drop-shadows.

2. **Typography & Readability**:
   - Modern geometric sans-serifs (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit).
   - High contrast hierarchy: `#0f172a` (Primary Slate), `#475569` (Secondary Slate), `#2369C9` (Accent Blue).
   - Monospace accents for telemetry, hex values, and C2 status indicators (`JetBrains Mono`, `Roboto Mono`).

3. **Motion & Feedback**:
   - Spring physics: Dampened springs (`damping: 22, stiffness: 280`) for natural, tactile responsiveness.
   - Ripple and pulse effects for active telemetry gauges (heartbeat pulses for online sentinels, glowing status pills).
   - Instant visual acknowledgment (<100ms) on all user actions.
